About the Ideathon

Why InsurTech, why now?

Insurance should protect everyone, but in Malaysia many people are still left out. In this Ideathon, student teams pitch fresh tech ideas across four themes.

01

AI Innovations for Insurance

Use AI to make insurance smarter, fairer and more affordable for everyone.

02

Claims & Underwriting Operations

Cut the paperwork and manual checks so claims and approvals happen in days, not weeks.

03

Future Distribution Models

Find new ways to reach B40 families and gig workers who are left uninsured today.

04

Human-Centric Customer Engagement

Build trust by keeping in touch with customers before something goes wrong.
