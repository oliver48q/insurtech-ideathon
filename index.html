About the Ideathon

Why InsurTech, why now?

Insurance should protect everyone, but in Malaysia many people are still left out. In this Ideathon, student teams pitch fresh tech ideas that close three big gaps.

01

Protection Gap

B40 families and gig workers are left uninsured because premiums are too high and too rigid.

02

Operational Friction

Claims still take 7 to 21 days, with lots of paperwork and manual checks.

03

Customer Disconnect

People only talk to their insurer when something goes wrong, so trust stays low.
